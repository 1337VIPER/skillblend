Looking for Blender…

Where is Blender installed?

SkillBlend uses your own copy of Blender (4.2 or newer) to read your files. Paste the path to blender.exe (Windows), the Blender app (Mac) or the blender program (Linux).

Show SkillBlend your work

Pick the folder where your Blender models live. SkillBlend looks through every file and learns how you build: your bevels, modifier stacks, SubD, booleans, kitbash pieces, materials, UVs and game-ready setup. Then it turns that into a skill, so Claude models the same way you do.

Your files are only read. They're never changed, saved or uploaded.
Keep your kitbash or insert library somewhere else? Add that folder too.
Backups, autosaves and folders starting with _ are skipped.
